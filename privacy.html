Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Jasola Rider is a free browser motorcycle game. This page explains what information the game and the services around it use, and what choices you have. You do not need an account to play.

What stays on your device

The game saves your best times, ghost recordings, chosen player name and settings in your browser's local storage, and short-lived multiplayer room details in session storage. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

Online multiplayer

If you use multiplayer, the game sends your chosen player name, room code and ride data (position, speed, lap progress and finish time) to a relay server run on Cloudflare Workers so other players in the same room can see you. This information is held in memory only while the room is open and is not stored afterwards. Cloudflare processes network details such as your IP address to deliver the connection. Please do not use your real name or personal details as a player name; other players in your room can see it.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and requested pages) for security and operation. We do not use these logs to identify you.

Third-party resources

The game loads fonts from Google Fonts and a 3D library from cdnjs (Cloudflare). Your browser contacts those services when the page loads, so they can see your IP address.

Advertising

We may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies, including to show personalised ads where the law allows. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ads that need cookies or personal data are shown only after you give consent through the consent message, and you can change your choice later from the same message. You can also manage personalised ads at adssettings.google.com and learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to or restrict its use. Because the game has no accounts, most data lives only in your own browser. For anything else, contact us using the contact details published on this site.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
